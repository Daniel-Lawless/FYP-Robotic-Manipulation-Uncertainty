## Current aims for the FYP

My aim is to investigate a focused, interesting research question while developing my understanding of reinforcement learning (RL) and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

By the end of the project, I want to be able to:

- Understand and explain the core principles of reinforcement learning and how they can be applied to robotic manipulation.
- Explain the architecture of a VLA model and how its visual, language and action components work together.
- Gain practical experience implementing or adapting these models and evaluating their behaviour through experiments.

I want to develop this understanding through a practical research project, with a clearly defined question that is feasible to investigate within the time and resources available.
